# Ingredient artwork provenance

These are original HomeChef vector illustrations created for the ingredient checklist.
They contain no third-party photographs, logos, fonts, or stock imagery.
The artwork in this directory is dedicated to the public domain under
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

Editable originals are in `sources/`; the matching 256-pixel PNGs are bundled
for offline native and web use. Render an SVG with Sharp at 256 × 256 to
reproduce its PNG. The basket is the shared fallback for unclassified items.
Artwork and factual display details never determine allergy or dietary safety.
